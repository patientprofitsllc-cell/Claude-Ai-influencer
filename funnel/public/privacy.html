This notice covers the Patient Creations project inquiry form.

What the form collects

Your business and contact name, email address, optional phone number, industry, revenue range, approximate customer value, optional website address, budget answer and contact preferences. The submission also records a timestamp and request identifier. Your IP address is temporarily used to limit repeated requests.

How it is used

Patient Creations uses this information to review your inquiry, determine a suitable next step and respond about the request. If you choose optional automated calls or texts, your information may be passed to a connected follow-up provider for that purpose. Optional marketing email preferences are recorded separately. Submitting the form does not itself reserve an appointment.

Storage and service providers

Requests are stored in the application’s database. Authorized operators can export them to review inquiries. When follow-up integrations are enabled, the submitted information and consent choices may be sent to the configured automation provider. Booking links open Cal.com, which handles information you enter under its own privacy terms.

Your choices

Automated calls, texts and marketing emails are optional. You may submit an inquiry and access booking without choosing those options. Reply STOP to stop automated texts or use the unsubscribe option in marketing emails. Contact Patient Creations through patientcreations.com to ask about your stored inquiry, correct it, or request deletion.

On your device

This form does not save your contact details in browser storage or set advertising cookies. Unsubmitted entries are held in the current page and may be lost if you reload it.
