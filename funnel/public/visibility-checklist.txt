PATIENT CREATIONS — LOCAL VISIBILITY CHECKLIST

Start with one check. Tick it only after you have verified it.

[ ] Business name, phone, address/service area and hours are accurate.
[ ] Your website opens cleanly on a phone.
[ ] The first screen says what you do and who you help.
[ ] Services have plain-language descriptions and useful next steps.
[ ] Contact buttons work. Test the phone, email and inquiry form.
[ ] Images show your work and you have permission to use them.
[ ] Your Google Business Profile is accurate and current.
[ ] The review link opens the right business profile.
[ ] Ask all customers for honest feedback without filtering by satisfaction.
[ ] Reply helpfully to feedback without exposing customer information.
[ ] Follow your booking or inquiry path from start to finish.
[ ] Write down one issue and fix it this week.

Weekly review: What changed? Did inquiries become easier to handle?
This is a practical checklist, not a promise of traffic or sales.
