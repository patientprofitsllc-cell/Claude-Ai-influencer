01

Read this first

This playbook gives you checks you can run yourself instead of advice you have to trust. It is for creators launching an AI-generated persona on Instagram and TikTok in the US, and every chapter names the raw source, the method, the tool and the test.

What it will not do

Promise followers, income or monetization approval. Earnings figures quoted here come from agency statements or third-party estimates and are not typical. Two questions are unresolved and marked where they appear: whether TikTok Creator Rewards accepts a labeled AI persona, and the exact TikTok Shop follower threshold.

How to use it

Work in order. Do not skip the compliance gate (chapter 5) or the asset audit (chapter 7). Most failed AI accounts break there, before the first post.

05

Compliance gate: pass this before the first post

Labeling is a reach decision, not a legal footnote. Since August 31, 2026, Instagram can limit the reach of profiles featuring an AI-generated person that skip its “AI-generated profile” label. Profiles that add it proactively see no change. TikTok requires labels on realistic AI content and auto-labels files carrying C2PA metadata; auto-applied labels cannot be removed.

Rules to know by name

The FTC Endorsement Guides define an endorser as someone who could be or appear to be an individual, and the 2023 revision covers virtual influencers. New York’s synthetic-performer ad disclosure law took effect June 9, 2026, and the EU AI Act’s Article 50 transparency duties apply from August 2, 2026 if you sell to EU audiences. This is general information, not legal advice; ask a lawyer before signing brand contracts.

Highest risks, in order

An unlabeled AI profile, sexualized or borderline imagery, templated near-identical AI clips, real-person likeness, watermarked cross-posts, and engagement pods or bought followers.

Lane test, three questions

Would a travel, fashion or consumer brand run this grid next to its product? Can you say in one sentence what a stranger gets by following? Does the offer you will sell fit the lane? Two or more “no” answers mean change the lane before you build content.

07

Audit your assets before posting

“120 posts” can mean 120 finished renders or 120 crops from one sheet, and the difference decides whether you can post at all. In one real archive, the read-me stated the files were crops from a single 1536×1024 contact sheet, resized with blurred padding. Grid lines and corrupted number labels showed in most crops, and the face and marks drifted between frames. Run these checks on every file before it goes on a schedule.

Six asset checks
CheckPass conditionHow to test it
Real resolutionDetail holds at 200% zoom; the source is not upscaledZoom in; check the pixel dimensions of the original render, not the export
Contact-sheet artifactsNo grid lines, numbers or labels in frameView at full size, not as a thumbnail
FramingSubject not cut off mid-body unless on purposeLook at the 4:5 and 9:16 crops separately
Identity consistencyFace, body, tattoos and glasses match the master referencePut ten random pairs side by side; any visible mismatch fails
Policy riskA hotel, travel or fashion brand would run it next to its productAsk it honestly; mark Low, Medium or High
OriginalityYour own render, not a reposted or watermarked fileCheck the file’s source and metadata

Sort the passing files into tiers by quality. Rebuild the best concepts first from one approved master reference, and use failing files only as pose and setting briefs. Do not post the failing ones to fill a calendar; they train the algorithm and your audience on the wrong face.

08

The 90 days, with kill criteria

Move to the next phase only when the gate is met, and act on the kill rule when it triggers. Every number is a starting threshold from creator-community practice, not a platform rule. Replace them with your own medians after 30 days.

Work. Launch the grid with three pinned posts; one TikTok video a day; trial Reels for every hook variant; test three series formats.

Gate. One format under a 35% skip rate; sends per reach above 0.5% on your top three posts.

Kill rule

Day 14: if no format holds the first three seconds across 10 posts, change the hook style, not the posting volume.

Work. Put 70% of posts in the winning series and number the episodes; launch the comment-to-DM lead magnet; add 60 to 90 second TikTok episodes; first Collab post.

Gate. 50 email opt-ins; non-follower reach above 50% on Reels; at least 1 follow per 1,000 views.

Kill rule

Day 45: if follows per 1,000 views stays under 0.5, add a series identity and a reason to follow before making more.

Work. Media kit; pitch 10 niche brands; turn on Gifts and TikTok Shop affiliate when eligible; release one digital product or paid tier.

Gate. A first paid dollar; two or more brand replies; 30-day follower growth rising.

Kill rule

Day 90: no first dollar means sell the offer to 10 real people by direct message before adding any new content.

Reply to every comment in the first hour. Leave 20 thoughtful comments a day as the persona in the first month, because early comments are your cheapest reach.

What AI personas have actually reported

Agency figures put Aitana Lopez’s average at about €3,000 a month with one month near €10,000, and over €1,000 per sponsored post at roughly 390,000 followers. An anonymous operator behind Emily Pellegrini reported about $23,000 in January 2024 on Fanvue after roughly $6,000 in October. These are early, press-driven results from first movers; a new account should not expect them.

11

The numbered-list reel formula

One format does most of the early work: a numbered list with a withheld payoff and a one-word comment keyword. Build every list reel the same way so you can compare results.

  1. Hook, first 1 to 2 seconds. A number, a promise and a parenthetical qualifier, for example “7 things to pack for a beach week (the last one fixes everything)”.
  2. List fills in on screen. Each item appears as you say it.
  3. One visual proof per item. About 1.4 seconds each; show the thing, not a stock clip.
  4. Withheld payoff. The best item or the fix lands last, so people watch to the end.
  5. One-word comment keyword. “Comment PACK for the free checklist.” The keyword triggers a DM with the lead-magnet pack and captures an email.
  6. Handle outro. Close on your handle.
  7. Safe zone. Keep key content in the middle of the frame, clear of platform UI at the top and bottom.

Weave in one of four hooks per script: a curiosity loop (open a question you close at the end), dead space (cut every silent frame), social proof (a number or result viewers can check), or us versus them (a common mistake against your fix).

Hook: 5 rooftop spots that look expensive (number 3 costs nothing)
Items: skyline bar | hotel terrace | free public deck | sunset pier | garden lounge
Payoff: number 3, the free one, appears last
CTA: Comment ROOF for the rooftop map
Outro: Follow [@handle] for the next list
Disclosure: AI-generated persona. Locations are real; scenes are generated.

Lead-magnet pack

One page the DM delivers: a checklist, a map or a template that matches the keyword. Every pack asks for an email in exchange. That list, not the follower count, is the asset you own.

Sources, caveats and disclaimer

Earnings and results disclaimer

This playbook does not guarantee followers, revenue or monetization approval. Income examples are agency statements or third-party estimates from early adopters and are not typical. Your results depend on your niche, execution, assets and platform decisions. This is general information, not legal or financial advice.

Caveats
  • Targets such as a 35% skip rate, 1% sends per reach and 1 follow per 1,000 views are creator-community heuristics, not figures Instagram or TikTok publish.
  • Platform rules on AI-generated profiles, originality and monetization changed several times in 2026. Recheck each official page every quarter.
  • Whether TikTok Creator Rewards accepts a labeled AI persona is unresolved, and TikTok Shop affiliate thresholds are reported inconsistently (1,000 versus 5,000).
  • A 2024 peer-reviewed study of 99,680 Instagram posts found human influencers earned more engagement than virtual ones, so plan for weaker per-post engagement and make it up with shareable formats.
