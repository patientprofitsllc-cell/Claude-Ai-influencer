# Deeper, not bigger.

Launch and monetize an AI influencer, with tests you can run yourself.

01

## Read this first

This playbook gives you checks you can run yourself instead of advice you have to trust. It is for creators launching an AI-generated persona on Instagram and TikTok in the US, and every chapter names the raw source, the method, the tool and the test.

### What it will not do

Promise followers, income or monetization approval. Earnings figures quoted here come from agency statements or third-party estimates and are not typical. Two questions are unresolved and marked where they appear: whether TikTok Creator Rewards accepts a labeled AI persona, and the exact TikTok Shop follower threshold.

### How to use it

Work in order. Do not skip the compliance gate (chapter 5) or the asset audit (chapter 7). Most failed AI accounts break there, before the first post.

## What a top operator does that most guides skip

Seven moves separate accounts that earn from accounts that only post. Most paid guides skip them because they are work, not secrets.

Seven moves top operators make

| Move | What a top operator does | Why it gets skipped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work backward from revenue | Sets a monthly revenue goal, then solves for the followers, views and conversions needed | A follower count sells better than a revenue model |
| Test demand before building the grid | Logs the last 90 days of 20 comparable accounts and studies posts at 5x or more of that account’s median views | It takes an afternoon of spreadsheet work |
| Audit assets before scheduling | Checks resolution, identity drift and policy risk on every image | Guides assume your files are good |
| Set kill criteria on day 1 | Writes down the numbers that mean change the series, change the lane, or stop | Nobody wants to plan for failure |
| Build the owned funnel first | Has an email capture, a lead magnet and one offer live before followers arrive | Followers feel like the goal |
| Treat labels and originality as ranking inputs | Labels the account, posts original files, and checks Account Status weekly | Disclosure feels like a cost, not a lever |
| Verify with outside signals | Uses stranger tests, brand replies and opt-in counts, not the creator’s own opinion | It is humbling |

## The method and tool stack

Each stage has one named method and the tools to run it. Swap tools freely; keep the method.

Method and tools for each stage

| Stage | Method | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demand research | Outlier analysis: log 20 comparable accounts, flag posts at 5x or more of median, code each hook and format | A spreadsheet, TikTok Creative Center, Google Trends, Instagram and TikTok search |
| Persona build | A written persona bible plus one approved master character reference (face from three angles, body, marks, glasses rule) | Any image generator that supports a locked character reference |
| Asset production | Render each concept individually from the master reference at 2K or higher; keep file metadata intact | Your generator, then a native editor per platform |
| Hook testing | Trial Reels: publish hook variants of the same asset to non-followers first | Instagram trial Reels, TikTok Studio |
| Funnel | Comment keyword triggers a DM with a lead-magnet pack, which captures an email | ManyChat or another API-approved DM tool, an email platform, a link hub such as Stan, Beacons or Linktree |
| Measurement | Weekly review of retention, sends per reach, follows per 1,000 views, non-follower share | Instagram Insights, TikTok Studio, exports through Metricool or Later |
| Pricing | Price sponsored work off average views, not followers | Collabstr rate listings, Meta Creator Marketplace |
| Compliance | Labels on, Account Status checked weekly, FTC disclosure template ready | Platform settings, the FTC guides |

## Compliance gate: pass this before the first post

Labeling is a reach decision, not a legal footnote. Since August 31, 2026, Instagram can limit the reach of profiles featuring an AI-generated person that skip its “AI-generated profile” label. Profiles that add it proactively see no change. TikTok requires labels on realistic AI content and auto-labels files carrying C2PA metadata; auto-applied labels cannot be removed.

- [ ] Instagram “AI-generated profile” label is on
- [ ] Every realistic AI post on TikTok carries the AI-generated content label
- [ ] File metadata is intact; you are not stripping C2PA or IPTC data
- [ ] Bio says AI in the first line, not behind a “more” link
- [ ] No real person’s likeness, no fake attendance at real events, no lookalike of an existing creator
- [ ] No fake personal testimonials; an AI persona cannot have used a product, so frame picks as recommendations
- [ ] Every paid or gifted post says #ad or uses the paid-partnership tag, in addition to any platform tag
- [ ] Every file is your own render; no reposted or watermarked clips
- [ ] Account Status on Instagram shows the account is recommendable

### Rules to know by name

The FTC Endorsement Guides define an endorser as someone who could be or appear to be an individual, and the 2023 revision covers virtual influencers. New York’s synthetic-performer ad disclosure law took effect June 9, 2026, and the EU AI Act’s Article 50 transparency duties apply from August 2, 2026 if you sell to EU audiences. This is general information, not legal advice; ask a lawyer before signing brand contracts.

### Highest risks, in order

An unlabeled AI profile, sexualized or borderline imagery, templated near-identical AI clips, real-person likeness, watermarked cross-posts, and engagement pods or bought followers.

## Audit your assets before posting

“120 posts” can mean 120 finished renders or 120 crops from one sheet, and the difference decides whether you can post at all. In one real archive, the read-me stated the files were crops from a single 1536×1024 contact sheet, resized with blurred padding. Grid lines and corrupted number labels showed in most crops, and the face and marks drifted between frames. Run these checks on every file before it goes on a schedule.

Six asset checks

| Check | Pass condition | How to test it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real resolution | Detail holds at 200% zoom; the source is not upscaled | Zoom in; check the pixel dimensions of the original render, not the export |
| Contact-sheet artifacts | No grid lines, numbers or labels in frame | View at full size, not as a thumbnail |
| Framing | Subject not cut off mid-body unless on purpose | Look at the 4:5 and 9:16 crops separately |
| Identity consistency | Face, body, tattoos and glasses match the master reference | Put ten random pairs side by side; any visible mismatch fails |
| Policy risk | A hotel, travel or fashion brand would run it next to its product | Ask it honestly; mark Low, Medium or High |
| Originality | Your own render, not a reposted or watermarked file | Check the file’s source and metadata |

Sort the passing files into tiers by quality. Rebuild the best concepts first from one approved master reference, and use failing files only as pose and setting briefs. Do not post the failing ones to fill a calendar; they train the algorithm and your audience on the wrong face.

## The 90 days, with kill criteria

Move to the next phase only when the gate is met, and act on the kill rule when it triggers. Every number is a starting threshold from creator-community practice, not a platform rule. Replace them with your own medians after 30 days.

**Work.** Launch the grid with three pinned posts; one TikTok video a day; trial Reels for every hook variant; test three series formats.

**Gate.** One format under a 35% skip rate; sends per reach above 0.5% on your top three posts.

**Kill rule**

Day 14: if no format holds the first three seconds across 10 posts, change the hook style, not the posting volume.

**Work.** Put 70% of posts in the winning series and number the episodes; launch the comment-to-DM lead magnet; add 60 to 90 second TikTok episodes; first Collab post.

**Gate.** 50 email opt-ins; non-follower reach above 50% on Reels; at least 1 follow per 1,000 views.

**Kill rule**

Day 45: if follows per 1,000 views stays under 0.5, add a series identity and a reason to follow before making more.

**Work.** Media kit; pitch 10 niche brands; turn on Gifts and TikTok Shop affiliate when eligible; release one digital product or paid tier.

**Gate.** A first paid dollar; two or more brand replies; 30-day follower growth rising.

**Kill rule**

Day 90: no first dollar means sell the offer to 10 real people by direct message before adding any new content.

Reply to every comment in the first hour. Leave 20 thoughtful comments a day as the persona in the first month, because early comments are your cheapest reach.

## The metrics that decide reach

Only three Instagram signals are confirmed by the platform’s own head: watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach, with sends weighing more for non-followers. Everything else is either a metric the app shows you or a creator-community target, and the table says which. Neither platform publishes signal weights, so ignore any “originality score” tier or “3 to 5 times a like” claim you see online.

Metrics, where to find them and starting targets

| Metric | Where to find it | Status | Starting target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sends (DM shares) per reach | Instagram post Insights | Confirmed top-3 | 1% or more |
| Watch time and average watch time | Reel Insights | Confirmed top-3 | 50% or more of length on short clips |
| Likes per reach | Insights | Confirmed top-3 | Track the trend |
| Skip rate in the first 3 seconds | Reel Insights | Platform metric | 35% or less |
| Retention chart | Reel and TikTok Studio | Platform metric | Cut or re-hook at the exact second of the drop |
| Non-follower share of reach | Insights, Audience | Platform metric | 50% or more on Reels |
| Profile visits to follows | Insights | Platform metric | Low means a weak bio, grid or pinned posts |
| TikTok traffic sources | TikTok Studio, video analytics | Platform metric | For You above 70% means algorithmic push; Search above 10% builds long tail |
| TikTok search queries | TikTok Studio | Platform metric | Write the next video for the exact query |
| Follows per 1,000 views | Your own calculation | Community | 1 or more |
| Account Status | Instagram Settings | Confirmed | Recommendable |

## Monetization ladder, with the unknowns marked

Platform tools pay late; owned and brand income starts early. Start the owned funnel in week 1, not at 10,000 followers.

**Unresolved: TikTok Creator Rewards**

No official text settles whether a labeled AI persona qualifies. Verify in TikTok Studio at 10,000 followers, and do not build the plan on it.

Monetization tools, thresholds and open questions

| Tool | Threshold (US) | Fit for an AI persona | Open question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Comment-to-DM lead magnet and email | None | Strong | None |
| Affiliate links (Amazon, LTK, ShopMy) | None to apply | Strong | Disclose every link |
| Instagram Gifts | 500 followers, 18+, professional account | Good | Small amounts |
| TikTok Shop affiliate | About 1,000 followers; some sources say 5,000 | Good | Confirm in TikTok Studio under Monetization |
| Brand and UGC deals | About 5,000 to 10,000 followers is a working benchmark | Good | Not a platform rule; price off average views |
| Instagram Subscriptions | 10,000 followers, 18+ | Good | Rollout can be gradual even when eligible |
| TikTok Creator Rewards | 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, original videos over 1 minute | Unresolved | The FAQ stresses content produced entirely by yourself, and a separate TikTok program’s terms say AI-generated content is not original content; no official text settles whether a labeled AI persona qualifies |
| TikTok LIVE | About 1,000 followers | Hard | A live avatar needs a rig and a human operator |
| Subscription platforms such as Fanvue or Patreon | Platform rules | Depends on lane | Fanvue’s best-known AI earners are adult-adjacent, which narrows brand work |

### What AI personas have actually reported

Agency figures put Aitana Lopez’s average at about €3,000 a month with one month near €10,000, and over €1,000 per sponsored post at roughly 390,000 followers. An anonymous operator behind Emily Pellegrini reported about $23,000 in January 2024 on Fanvue after roughly $6,000 in October. These are early, press-driven results from first movers; a new account should not expect them.

## The numbered-list reel formula

One format does most of the early work: a numbered list with a withheld payoff and a one-word comment keyword. Build every list reel the same way so you can compare results.

1. **Hook, first 1 to 2 seconds.** A number, a promise and a parenthetical qualifier, for example “7 things to pack for a beach week (the last one fixes everything)”.
2. **List fills in on screen.** Each item appears as you say it.
3. **One visual proof per item.** About 1.4 seconds each; show the thing, not a stock clip.
4. **Withheld payoff.** The best item or the fix lands last, so people watch to the end.
5. **One-word comment keyword.** “Comment PACK for the free checklist.” The keyword triggers a DM with the lead-magnet pack and captures an email.
6. **Handle outro.** Close on your handle.
7. **Safe zone.** Keep key content in the middle of the frame, clear of platform UI at the top and bottom.

Weave in one of four hooks per script: a curiosity loop (open a question you close at the end), dead space (cut every silent frame), social proof (a number or result viewers can check), or us versus them (a common mistake against your fix).

```
Hook: 5 rooftop spots that look expensive (number 3 costs nothing)
Items: skyline bar | hotel terrace | free public deck | sunset pier | garden lounge
Payoff: number 3, the free one, appears last
CTA: Comment ROOF for the rooftop map
Outro: Follow [@handle] for the next list
Disclosure: AI-generated persona. Locations are real; scenes are generated.
```

### Lead-magnet pack

One page the DM delivers: a checklist, a map or a template that matches the keyword. Every pack asks for an email in exchange. That list, not the follower count, is the asset you own.

## Sources, caveats and disclaimer

Earnings and results disclaimer

This playbook does not guarantee followers, revenue or monetization approval. Income examples are agency statements or third-party estimates from early adopters and are not typical. Your results depend on your niche, execution, assets and platform decisions. This is general information, not legal or financial advice.

Caveats

- Targets such as a 35% skip rate, 1% sends per reach and 1 follow per 1,000 views are creator-community heuristics, not figures Instagram or TikTok publish.
- Platform rules on AI-generated profiles, originality and monetization changed several times in 2026. Recheck each official page every quarter.
- Whether TikTok Creator Rewards accepts a labeled AI persona is unresolved, and TikTok Shop affiliate thresholds are reported inconsistently (1,000 versus 5,000).
- A 2024 peer-reviewed study of 99,680 Instagram posts found human influencers earned more engagement than virtual ones, so plan for weaker per-post engagement and make it up with shareable formats.
